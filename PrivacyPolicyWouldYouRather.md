# Would You Rather — Privacy Policy


**Effective date:** 20, August 2026

Would You Rather ("the app") is developed by OSDev ("we," "us"). This policy explains what happens — and, mostly, what doesn't — with your information when you use the app.

## 1. Overview

Would You Rather works entirely offline. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, no backend server, and does not transmit your gameplay, your preferences, or any content you see in the app back to us. Everything the app remembers about you — your theme choice, accent color, language, and whether you've confirmed you're 18+ — stays on your device.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to:
- Your name, email address, or any account information (there is no account).
- Your gameplay activity, answers, or history.
- Any content you view in the app.
- Your precise location.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad, served by Google AdMob. To do this, Google's advertising SDK may collect and process:
- Device and advertising identifiers (such as your device's advertising ID).
- General, approximate location (typically derived from IP address, not GPS).
- App interaction data used for ad delivery, frequency capping, and measurement.

This collection is performed by Google as an advertising vendor, not by us directly, and is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Google's AdMob data-use terms. We do not receive or store this data ourselves.

## 4. Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland (GDPR)

If you're located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a consent prompt (via Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time you open it, before any personalized ads are requested. You can review or withdraw your consent choices at any time from **Settings → Privacy Options**.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Would You Rather is not directed at children. The app includes an Adult 18+ content category, which sits behind an in-app age confirmation before it can be opened. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age applicable in your region (commonly 13, or 16 in parts of the EEA). If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us using the details in §8 — though given the app's fully offline design, this would most likely involve Google's advertising SDK rather than anything we hold ourselves.

## 6. Data Storage & Security

The handful of preferences the app stores — theme, accent color, language, and your Adult 18+ confirmation — live in local, on-device storage only. They are never transmitted to us or to any third party, with the sole exception of the advertising and consent data described in §3–4, which Google's SDKs handle directly on your device.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because Would You Rather doesn't hold any personal data on our servers — we don't have any servers — most of these rights are automatically satisfied by the app's design. The one that's genuinely actionable is withdrawing ad consent, which you can do anytime via **Settings → Privacy Options**. For anything else, or for questions about Google's handling of advertising data, see §3.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
