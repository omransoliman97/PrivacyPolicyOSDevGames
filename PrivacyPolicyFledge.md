# Fledge — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 29, 2026

Fledge ("the game", "the app") is an arrow-escape puzzle for Android and iOS, published by **OSDev** ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and what choices you have. We wrote it to be short and readable.

**In one paragraph:** Fledge has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases. Your progress is saved only on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, uses Firebase Analytics to understand how levels are played (only after you have had the chance to give consent where the law requires it), and can send you announcements through Firebase Cloud Messaging. We do not sell your personal information.

## 1. Who is responsible

OSDev is responsible for the game. You can reach us at **omransoliman.osdevapps@gmail.com** for anything related to this policy.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The game keeps a small save file in the app's private storage. It contains:

- your campaign progress, stars, hints, streaks, Daily Puzzle history and Collection;
- your settings (theme, accent color, language, sound, music, haptics, rules and other options);
- a short diagnostic log used to help recover from errors.

This information stays on your device. We do not receive it and we cannot read it. It is removed when you uninstall the app or choose **Settings › Delete all data**. Because there is no account or cloud backup, deleted or uninstalled progress cannot be restored.

## 3. Information handled by third-party services

The game includes the following services. Each one has its own privacy policy, linked below.

| Service | What it is used for | Information it may process |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK) | Showing a banner ad and optional rewarded videos | Advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and operating-system information, app version, language, ad interactions, diagnostic and performance data |
| **Google User Messaging Platform** | Asking for your consent to ads and measurement where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) | Your consent choices and the technical data needed to store them |
| **Firebase Analytics** | Understanding which levels are played and where players get stuck, so we can improve the game | An app-instance identifier, device model, operating-system version, app version, country or region, and the game events listed below |
| **Firebase Cloud Messaging** | Optional notifications and announcements from us | A Firebase installation identifier or registration token, the notification permission state, and a subscription to a general announcements topic |
| **Google Play / Apple App Store** | Downloading the app and the optional store review prompt | Handled by the store under its own terms. The game does not receive your name, email or account |

**Game events sent to Firebase Analytics** (only when analytics is enabled, see section 4): level started (level name and mode), level finished (level name, mode, success, stars, mistakes, time in seconds), hint used (level name), out of hearts (level name), and rewarded video completed (hint or heart). These events contain no name, email, phone number or other direct identifier.

**Ads.** Ads are limited to a maximum content rating of "PG". The game never interrupts you with full-screen ads between levels or during play. Rewarded videos are optional and only start when you tap the button for a hint or an extra heart. Google may use the information above to show you ads that are relevant to you, to limit how often you see an ad, and to prevent fraud and abuse. See Google's explanation at <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

**Notifications.** On Android 13 and later and on iOS the system asks for permission before notifications are shown. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

## 4. Consent and your choices

- **Consent form.** Where the law requires it, the game shows Google's consent form before ads and analytics start, and Google's SDKs follow the choices you make there. If you decline or limit your choices, for example, ads are not personalised and measurement is limited to what your choices allow. When the form is available again, a **Privacy options** row appears in Settings so you can change your mind.
- **Timing.** Ads, analytics and notifications are initialised only after you have played the first few levels.
- **Advertising ID.** On Android you can reset it or opt out of ad personalisation in *Settings › Google › Ads*. On iOS you control tracking in *Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking*.
- **Notifications.** Turn them off in your device's notification settings.
- **Delete local data.** Use **Settings › Delete all data** or uninstall the app.
- **Review prompt.** The review dialog is shown by Google Play or Apple. We do not receive your review account details.

## 5. Why we use information (legal bases)

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on:

- **your consent** for ads personalisation, analytics and notifications;
- **our legitimate interests** in operating, securing and improving the game and preventing fraud, balanced against your rights.

## 6. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers listed in section 3, who process it to provide their services. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with data brokers. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights.

## 7. International transfers

Google processes information in many countries, including outside your own. Where required, Google relies on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. See Google's privacy policy for details.

## 8. Retention

- Save-file data stays on your device until you delete it.
- Analytics data is kept by Google according to the retention period configured for the Firebase project and then deleted or aggregated.
- Advertising data is kept by Google as described in its policies.

## 9. Children

Fledge is a general-audience puzzle game and is **not directed to children under 13** (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help get it removed from our side and point you to the right place at Google.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA or Brazil's LGPD), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your information, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data-protection authority.

We do not hold names, emails or accounts, so most information about you sits with Google. To exercise your rights you can:

1. use the in-app **Privacy options** and your device's advertising and notification settings;
2. use Google's tools at <https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy>; and
3. email us at **omransoliman.osdevapps@gmail.com**. We will answer within a reasonable time and, where we cannot act ourselves, tell you who can.

**California and other US states.** We do not sell personal information for money. Some advertising uses may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under state laws. You can opt out with your device's advertising settings or the in-app Privacy options when shown.

## 11. Security

We rely on the security of the platform and of Google's SDKs, which transmit data over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
