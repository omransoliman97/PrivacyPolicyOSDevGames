# Aloft privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Aloft ("the app") is a game published by **[OSDev]**. Contact: **[omransoliman.osdevapps@gmail.com]**.

## The short version

- Aloft has **no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no crash-reporting service**.
- Your progress, settings and unlocked items are stored **only on your device**. We never
  receive them. Aloft has no real-money purchases.
- The app shows ads from **Google AdMob**. Google's advertising SDK, not Aloft, collects the
  information described under "Ads" below. You can say no to personalised ads.
- Gameplay works fully offline. The network is used only to load ads and the consent form.

## What stays on your device

Aloft saves your best scores, Sparks, unlocked cosmetics, missions, daily streak, settings and
the state of the ad rules (for example when the last interstitial was shown) in the app's private
storage. This data never leaves the device through Aloft.

If your device backup is on, the operating system may include this storage in its own backup
(Android Auto Backup to your Google account, or an iOS device backup). That backup is controlled
by you and your platform provider, not by us.

You can erase everything at any time: **Settings → Reset progress**, or uninstall the app.

## Ads

Aloft is free and supported by ads shown through **Google AdMob** (banner, interstitial and
rewarded video ads). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect and
use, on our behalf and under Google's own policies:

- your advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, iOS IDFA if you allow tracking);
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
- device and app information (model, operating-system version, language, app version);
- how you interact with ads;
- diagnostics, such as crashes and performance of the ad SDK.

**Your choices**

- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions, a consent form from
  Google's User Messaging Platform appears the first time you open the app. You can change your
  answer at any time in **Settings → Privacy & ad choices**.
- On iOS you may also be asked, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether Aloft
  may track you across other companies' apps and websites. Declining does not limit gameplay.
- On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation in the
  system settings (**Settings → Privacy → Ads**, wording varies by device).

More about how Google uses data from apps that use its services:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites> and
<https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543>.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Internet and network state (Android) | Load ads and the consent form. Not needed to play. |
| Advertising ID (Android `AD_ID`) | Lets AdMob show and measure ads. You can reset it or opt out in system settings. |

Aloft does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any account.

## What the app opens outside itself

- **Share Aloft** opens your system share sheet with a link to the store page. Nothing is sent
  unless you pick an app and send it.
- **Rate Aloft** shows the store's own rating prompt or page.
- **Privacy policy** and **Our other apps** open your browser or the store.

## Children

Aloft is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an
ad or any other route, contact us and we will help you resolve it.

## Retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data held by Google
for advertising is governed by Google's policies and your Google account's ad settings.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version replaces this page and the date above is updated.
