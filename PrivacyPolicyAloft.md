## The short version

- Aloft has **no accounts and no sign-in**. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, photos, contacts or location.
- Your progress, settings and unlocked items stay **on your device**. Aloft has no real-money purchases.
- The app uses three Google services: **AdMob** (ads), **Google Analytics for Firebase** (usage statistics) and **Firebase Cloud Messaging** (notifications, only if you turn them on). They process the data described below.
- **You choose.** Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) a consent form appears the first time you open the app, and ads and statistics start only after it. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy & ad choices**. Notifications are **off** until you switch them on in **Settings → Notifications**, and you can switch them off again at any time.
- Gameplay works fully offline. The network is used for ads, the consent form, statistics and notifications.

## Who is responsible

OSDev (contact above) is responsible for what the app itself does. Google is responsible for the data its own services process, as described in its policies (links at the end). Because the app has no accounts, we cannot tell who you are from the data we can see.

## What stays on your device

Aloft saves your best scores, Sparks, unlocked cosmetics, missions, daily streak, settings and the state of the ad rules (for example when the last interstitial was shown) in the app's private storage. We do not receive this data.

If your device backup is on, the operating system may include this storage in its own backup (Android Auto Backup to your Google account, or an iOS device backup). That backup is controlled by you and your platform provider, not by us.

You can erase everything at any time: **Settings → Reset progress**, or uninstall the app.

## What is processed, why and on what basis

| Service | What | Why | When it starts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ads: Google AdMob | Advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, how you interact with ads, SDK diagnostics | Show and measure ads, prevent fraud | After the consent flow, where one is required; ads stop if you decline |
| Statistics: Google Analytics for Firebase | An app-instance ID, the Android advertising ID, approximate location from a masked IP address, device and app information (model, operating-system version, language, app version), and the events listed below | Understand how the game is used and improve it | After the consent flow, where one is required; they stop if you withdraw consent |
| Notifications: Firebase Cloud Messaging | A registration token (a random identifier for the app on your device) and the technical details needed to deliver messages (app version, platform). On iOS, Apple's push service delivers the messages | Send you news and reminders from Aloft | Only after you switch Notifications on in Settings; the token is deleted when you switch it off |

Where the GDPR or the UK GDPR applies, we rely on your **consent** (Article 6(1)(a)) for ads, statistics and notifications. Elsewhere we process this data as local law and Google's terms allow.

### Ads (Google AdMob)

Aloft is free and supported by ads shown through **Google AdMob** (banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads). Ads appear only between runs, never during play, and every rewarded video is your choice. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect and use, on our behalf and under Google's own policies:

- your advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, iOS IDFA if you allow tracking);
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
- device and app information (model, operating-system version, language, app version);
- how you interact with ads;
- diagnostics, such as crashes and performance of the ad SDK.

### Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Besides the automatic events (first open, session start, app updates), Aloft records these events: screen views (Home, Climb, Locker, Missions, Settings and so on), level start and level end (level number, success or not, game mode), score, tutorial completed, and a run summary (mode, level reached, altitude, Sparks earned, run length in seconds). None of them contains your name or contact details. Google keeps event-level data for the retention period set in Google Analytics (2 months by default).

### Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

If you turn Notifications on, Aloft asks the system for permission and registers your device so that we can send news and reminders. A message can show only while Aloft is in the background. Switching Notifications off in Settings removes your device from our message list and deletes the registration token. You can also turn notifications off in your system settings.

## Your choices

- **Consent form.** In the EEA, the UK and some other regions Google's User Messaging Platform shows a consent form the first time you open the app. Change your answer at any time in **Settings → Privacy & ad choices**.
- **iOS tracking.** On iOS you may be asked, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether Aloft may track you across other companies' apps and websites. Declining does not limit gameplay.
- **Android advertising ID.** You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation in the system settings (**Settings → Privacy → Ads**, wording varies by device).
- **Notifications.** **Settings → Notifications** (off by default), or your system settings.
- **Erase local data.** **Settings → Reset progress**, or uninstall the app.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Internet and network state (Android) | Load ads, the consent form, statistics and notifications. Not needed to play. |
| Advertising ID (Android `AD_ID`) | Lets AdMob and Google Analytics use the advertising ID. You can reset it or opt out in system settings. |
| Notifications (Android 13 and later: `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`; iOS: notification permission) | Asked only when you switch Notifications on in Settings. |
| Tracking (iOS App Tracking Transparency) | Asked through the consent flow, only where ads need it. |

Aloft does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any account.

## Sharing and transfers

We do not sell your data and we do not give it to anyone but the services named above. Google may process data in the United States and other countries; where the law requires it, Google relies on approved safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses (see Google's terms).

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA) you may have the right to access, correct or erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting what happened before) and to complain to your data protection authority. Because the app has no accounts, the identifiers it uses belong to your device and we cannot look you up by name. Write to us at the address above and we will help as far as we can; you can also use Google's own tools (your Google Account's ad settings, and resetting your advertising ID).

## Children

Aloft is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. In some countries the age at which you may consent to data processing is higher than 13 (up to 16); if you are below it, please ask a parent or guardian before you accept the consent form or turn on Notifications. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an ad or any other route, contact us and we will help you resolve it.

## Retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you ourselves. Data processed by Google is kept under Google's policies and your Google account's settings. Local data on your device is kept until you reset progress or uninstall the app.

## Security

Data sent by the Google SDKs travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
