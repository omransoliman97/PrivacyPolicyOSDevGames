# Privacy Policy for Truth or Dare

**Last updated:** October 25, 2026

## Overview
Truth or Dare ("we", "us", "our"), developed by OSDev, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Truth or Dare collects, how it's used, and your choices — for both the app and this policy itself.

## Information We Collect
- **Device and advertising information**: To deliver and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect device advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID / Apple's IDFA), your IP address, device model, OS version, app version, language and time-zone settings, and information about how you interact with ads (such as impressions and clicks). Whether and how this happens depends on your consent choices — see "Your Consent Choices" below.
- **Consent choices**: Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), Truth or Dare shows Google's consent form when you open the app. Your choices are recorded by Google's consent SDK on your device so you aren't asked every time; we don't receive a copy of them.
- **Usage and analytics data**: Truth or Dare uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and to improve it. It collects basic app events (such as first open and session start), the screens you open, how many games you finish and in which game mode, your app version, device model, operating system version and language setting, a random identifier for this installation of the app (the app-instance ID / Firebase installation ID), an advertising identifier where your device allows it, and an approximate location (country or city level) derived from your IP address. It does not collect your name, your email address, the player names or photos you enter, or your precise location. Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), analytics only starts after you agree in Google's consent form, and it stops if you decline or later withdraw your consent — see "Your Consent Choices" below.
- **Notification data**: If you allow notifications, Truth or Dare uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send them. This involves a notification token (an identifier Google creates for this installation of the app so that a message can be delivered to it), the Firebase installation ID, your app version, and basic device information such as the operating system version and device model. On iOS, the token is also tied to Apple's push notification service. We only register your device for notifications after you allow them, and we don't use the token to identify you.
- **On-device counters**: The app keeps simple counters on your device — how many games you've finished, and whether it has already asked about notifications. It uses them only to decide when ads may start appearing and when to ask about notifications; they never leave your device.
- **Location**: We do not collect your precise location. Google AdMob and Google Analytics may infer a general (approximate) location from your IP address, to serve ads and to produce usage statistics.
- **User content**: Truth or Dare stores the player names and optional photos you enter for each game mode. This is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us or any server. If you use the app's Import/Export feature, the resulting file is created and shared entirely at your own direction — we don't receive a copy of it.
- **Account information**: Truth or Dare does not require an account and stores no personal account information.

## How We Use Information
We use the information above to operate the app on your device; to serve and measure ads through our ad partner, Google AdMob; to understand how the app is used and improve it, through Google Analytics for Firebase; to send you notifications if you've allowed them, through Firebase Cloud Messaging; and to honor your consent choices. We do not use it for any other purpose.

## Advertising
Truth or Dare is free and supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. You may see banner ads, full-screen (interstitial) ads — for example after a game ends — app open ads when you come back to the app after being away for a while, and optional rewarded ads before a game in the Adult +18 mode. These ads are chosen and delivered by Google and its partners, who may use the identifiers described above to show ads that are more relevant to you where you have allowed it. We don't control the content of third-party ads.

## Notifications
Truth or Dare can send you notifications — for example to tell you about updates or new content. The app asks for your permission once, after you finish your first game, and we only register your device for notifications if you allow them; that permission is your consent for us to send them. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device's system settings, and you can play the game either way.

## Your Consent Choices (EEA, UK & Switzerland)
If you're in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform). Consent is the legal basis (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and the ePrivacy rules) for storing or accessing information on your device and for using it to personalize ads. You can accept, decline, or manage your choices when the form appears, and you can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy Options (shown when your region requires it) or in your device's advertising settings. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect processing that took place before you withdrew it. The same choice covers Google Analytics for Firebase: where consent is required, analytics stays off until you agree, and it stops again if you decline or withdraw your consent. If you don't give consent, ads may be limited or not shown at all, but the game itself still works.

## Data Sharing & Disclosure
We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the third-party services listed above, as required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of Truth or Dare, our users, or the public.

## Data Retention
Player names and photos you enter remain on your device until you remove them individually, use the in-app "Delete All Data" option, or uninstall the app. We don't store this information on our own servers, so there's no separate server-side retention period to disclose. Device advertising identifiers used by Google AdMob persist until you reset them at the OS level (Settings → Privacy → Tracking on iOS, or Settings → Ads on Android). The on-device counters (finished games, and whether the app has asked about notifications) are kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics for a limited period — no longer than 14 months — and is then deleted automatically. A notification token is kept until you uninstall the app or clear its data, or until Firebase expires it after a long period of inactivity.

## Your Rights & Choices
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your information, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw your consent at any time. If you're in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in France, the CNIL — www.cnil.fr). If you live in a US state with a comprehensive privacy law (such as California), you may also have the right to opt out of targeted advertising and of the "sharing" of personal information for it; we don't sell personal information for money.

Since your player data lives only on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app via Settings → Delete All Data. You can change your advertising and analytics consent in Settings → Privacy Options (shown when your region requires it), you can turn notifications off at any time in your device's system settings, and you can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Ads / Tracking settings. For information Google collects for ads, you can also use Google's own tools at https://myadcenter.google.com. Outside regions where the consent form is shown, analytics is on by default; you can stop all collection of information by the app by uninstalling it. To reach us for anything else, contact us at omransoliman.osdevapps@gmail.com.

## Children's Privacy
Truth or Dare is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app may be used by a mixed-age audience; we do not knowingly serve behavioral or interest-based advertising to users who identify as under 13. If we learn we've collected personal information from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will delete it promptly. A parent or guardian who believes their child has provided us with personal information can contact us at omransoliman.osdevapps@gmail.com to request its deletion. Where local law sets a higher age for giving consent to personalized advertising, a parent or guardian should help with the consent choices.

## Age-Restricted Content
Truth or Dare includes an optional 18+ mode containing content intended only for users 18 and older. Access requires confirming you meet this age requirement, and we do not knowingly grant access to users who identify as under 18. Depending on your region, additional age-verification steps beyond self-confirmation may be required by local law before this content can be offered — confirm current requirements for each region this content will be available in before launch.
